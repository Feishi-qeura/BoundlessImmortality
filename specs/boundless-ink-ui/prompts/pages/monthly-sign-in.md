# 月签到

## 页面定位
展示服务端月度签到格、累计节点和可领取状态；与每日签运页分开。

## 视觉约束
720×1280 竖屏，暖宣纸、墨绿、青玉分隔与少量旧金；日历格清楚可点，SafeZone 24，触控格至少 72×72。不得把客户端当前日期当权威日期。

## 布局与组件
顶部固定返回、标题与服务端月份；中段七列月历，区分已签、今日、未到、补签关闭；下段累计进度与 3/7/14/21 节点奖励；底部固定可领取 CTA 或状态说明。给跨月和月份切换留出位置。

## 状态
加载、月历就绪、无数据、漏签、补签关闭、节点未达/可领/已领、断线快照、领取中与失败。

## 图像生成提示词
Create one portrait 9:16, 720x1280 xianxia mobile RPG monthly sign-in UI mockup. Use the approved warm ivory xuan-paper and restrained ink-green/jade/antique-gold style. Show a fixed back/title header and blank server-month field; a readable seven-column calendar with 5–6 rows and distinct visual markers for claimed, today, future, and missed days; a cumulative progress strip with four reward milestones positioned at days 3, 7, 14, and 21; a small selected reward detail panel; and a fixed bottom claim/status area. Use sparse paper-cloud ornament only around edges, keep all dates and reward names as blank type areas for later rendering, with no client-generated date or fake currency. Orthographic front-on UI, clear mobile touch targets and safe areas, no phone frame.

## 反向限制
No local screenshots as reference, no 28-day reward milestone, no fortune reroll, no invented rewards/quantities, no unlabelled state conveyed only by color, no tiny calendar, no missing 3/7/14/21 alignment, no gibberish, watermark, logo or neon UI.
